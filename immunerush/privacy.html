Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the game Immune Rush for iPhone and iPad (the "app") handles your data. The short version: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to any server operated by us or by third parties.

Data stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves the following on your device only, using Apple's standard app storage: earned DNA, unlocked cells, Lab upgrades, best times, and your settings (sound, vibration, damage numbers). This data never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app or tap "Reset progress" in Settings. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include it in your backups under Apple's own privacy terms.

Children

The app is suitable for players of all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. When you download the app, Apple processes data as described in Apple's privacy policy; we receive only the anonymous, aggregated statistics Apple shows to all developers in App Store Connect.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to answer it and delete it when it is no longer needed. Under the GDPR you may contact us at any time and complain to a data protection authority.

This support website

The app's device-only storage is separate from visiting this website. This static support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added website analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact forms.

Changes

If the app ever starts to collect data (for example through optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will change accordingly.
